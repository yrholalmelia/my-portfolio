Career Direction

Turning today’s learning into tomorrow’s impact.

A practical roadmap for growing from an IT student into a capable, adaptable software professional.

Short-Term • 1–2 Years

Build a strong technical foundation

Complete my IT studies with a portfolio of responsive websites, database projects, networking labs, and beginner full-stack applications.

  • Strengthen JavaScript, SQL, Git, and responsive UI development.
  • Build at least 3 polished projects with clear documentation.
  • Gain internship, freelance, or entry-level project experience.
Mid-Term • 3–5 Years

Become a dependable software engineer

Grow into a professional role where I can design, build, test, and maintain production-quality applications with a collaborative team.

  • Develop strong front-end and backend capabilities.
  • Work with APIs, cloud services, CI/CD, and databases.
  • Improve architecture, testing, code review, and team leadership skills.
Long-Term • 7–10 Years

Lead meaningful technology solutions

Progress toward a senior or technical-lead role where I can guide projects, mentor others, and help build reliable systems with real user value.

  • Lead end-to-end software projects and technical decisions.
  • Mentor junior developers and contribute to engineering standards.
  • Stay current with emerging technology while keeping solutions practical.

Preferred Domain

Fintech & Digital Services

I am interested in technology-driven services where reliability, security, and user experience matter. Fintech is especially appealing because it combines software engineering, data, cybersecurity, and real-world business impact.

What Drives Me

Curiosity, growth, and usefulness

My motivation comes from learning how systems work, solving problems step by step, and creating tools that make work or everyday tasks easier. I value continuous improvement, responsible technology, teamwork, and the confidence that comes from building things that actually work.
